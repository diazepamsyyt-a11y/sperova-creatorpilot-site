SPEROVA CreatorPilot is a local companion for Codex that helps creators manage their own YouTube channels, organize publishing and plan improvements from available performance data.

在本机管理自己的频道，查看表现、整理发布工作，并根据真实数据制定下一步经营计划。当前为本地使用版本，尚未开放公共注册。

Explore the workflow ↓
01 / UNDERSTAND

See what changed.

Review channel totals, video performance and the latest available 28-day analytics. Cached results retain their source and reporting dates.

02 / ORGANIZE

Keep publishing in order.

Prepare uploads, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and schedules. Changes follow the account owner’s instructions.

03 / PLAN

Make the next video count.

Use channel evidence to compare topics and packaging, identify missing data and prepare production briefs. A suggestion does not publish a video.

A deliberate workflow

Connect. Review. Decide.

  1. Install the local assistant on your Mac or Windows computer.
  2. Authorize a channel through Google, then check and confirm its identity.
  3. Review available data and choose the changes you want to make.

This website explains the product. It does not sign you in, connect to your channels or collect your credentials.

Access that matches the task.

Publishing access supports the operations you request. Read-only analytics access is separately authorized. Optional Google Cloud Monitoring access is used only to read project quota usage. You can revoke access at any time.

Data is cached locally. Automatic refresh runs while the local assistant is running and the computer is awake; Google’s reporting delay and API limits still apply. Current metrics may differ from YouTube Studio’s estimates.

授权按用途区分，数据保存在本机。自动更新依赖程序运行、有效授权和平台可用数据；不会承诺实时同步或自动恢复已被 Google 撤销的授权。
